# UNIVERSITY ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 2026-03

### AN ORDER PRESCRIBING THE RULES AND PROCEDURES GOVERNING THE RESERVATION OF SHARED CAMPUS FACILITIES THROUGH THE CAMPUSROOM SYSTEM

---

**WHEREAS**, the reservation of shared academic spaces — study rooms, computer laboratories, lecture halls, and other bookable facilities of the University — has historically been conducted through manual logbooks and informal requests, resulting in frequent double-bookings, undocumented approvals, and the absence of a reliable record for maintenance planning and accountability;

**WHEREAS**, the University has commissioned and deployed CampusRoom, a centralized, role-based information system, to receive, evaluate, approve, and record all such reservations electronically;

**WHEREAS**, it is necessary to formally prescribe the rules under which CampusRoom shall operate, so that no expectation, exception, or practice exists outside of what this Order provides;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the following rules and procedures are hereby ordered to take effect for all use of the CampusRoom system:

---

## ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

**Section 1.** For purposes of this Order:

(a) **"Requestor"** refers to the holder of a Customer account who submits a reservation request in their own name and remains accountable for it.

(b) **"Approving Authority"** refers to any account holding the Staff or Admin role, empowered to approve, reject, or otherwise act upon a reservation request.

(c) **"Facility"** or **"Room"** refers to any bookable space registered in the system's Rooms table.

(d) **"Business Day"** refers to Monday through Saturday, exclusive of any date entered in the Holidays table.

(e) **"Class Schedule"** refers to a recurring weekly academic commitment registered against a Facility, independent of the reservation system.

---

## ARTICLE II — ELIGIBILITY TO REQUEST A RESERVATION

**Section 1.** Only a holder of an authenticated Customer account may submit a new reservation request. No provision of this Order shall be construed to permit a Staff or Admin account to submit a reservation in its own name.

**Section 2.** Where a Staff or Admin account initiates a reservation on behalf of a Requestor through the re-filing procedure (Article XIII), the resulting reservation shall be recorded under, and shall remain the accountability of, the original Requestor, and not the initiating Staff or Admin account.

**Section 3.** No person without a University-registered account may access the CampusRoom system in any capacity, including the browsing of facility availability.

---

## ARTICLE III — CLASSIFICATION OF RESERVATIONS

**Section 1.** Every reservation request shall be filed under exactly one of the following classifications, and no other:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(i) Academic Lecture
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(ii) Faculty Defense
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(iii) Student Org Meeting
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(iv) Dept Workshop
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(v) Exam/Quiz

**Section 2.** A request filed under the classification "Student Org Meeting" should state, within its description, the name of the sponsoring organization and its faculty adviser. This requirement is one of disclosure only; the system does not withhold approval on account of its omission (see Article XV).

**Section 3.** No classification exists for departments, external vendors, or bodies unaffiliated with the University. A reservation shall in every case be filed by, and classified as pertaining to, a University-affiliated Requestor.

---

## ARTICLE IV — GUESTS AND NON-UNIVERSITY ATTENDEES

**Section 1.** Persons who are not holders of a University account may attend an event held under a reservation, but shall in no case be the Requestor of record for that reservation.

**Section 2.** Where guests are expected, the Requestor should so state within the reservation's description, including an approximate count.

**Section 3.** The Requestor shall at all times remain accountable for the conduct of their guests within the reserved Facility, for the whole of the reserved period, without regard to the classification under which the reservation was filed.

---

## ARTICLE IV-A — EQUIPMENT AND TECHNICAL SETUP

**Section 1.** A Requestor may declare, at the time of filing, the audio-visual or technical apparatus expected to be required within the Facility. Such declaration is optional; where none is made, the reservation shall be understood to call for the standard academic setup of the Facility as it stands.

**Section 2.** A declaration under Section 1 is a notice to the Approving Authority and to facilities personnel. The system does not reserve, allocate, or track the apparatus named; it records the declaration and displays it, and nothing further.

**Section 3.** The declaration is recorded against the reservation and carried forward upon re-filing under Article XIII.

---

## ARTICLE V — HOURS OF OPERATION AND FACILITY AVAILABILITY

**Section 1.** No Facility may be reserved except between the hours of **7:30 in the morning and 9:00 in the evening.**

**Section 2.** No Facility may be reserved on a Sunday, nor on any date entered in the Holidays table.

**Section 3.** A Facility marked under Maintenance status, or otherwise deactivated, shall not be reservable under any circumstance, for any hour or date, until such time as an Admin account restores it to active, Available status.

**Section 4.** No procedure exists within this system for the extension of operating hours, and no request for such extension shall be entertained, regardless of the classification, role, or urgency asserted.

---

## ARTICLE VI — PRECEDENCE OF ACADEMIC SCHEDULES

**Section 1.** A Facility committed to a Class Schedule shall not be reserved during the day and hour of that commitment, by any Requestor, without exception.

**Section 2.** Where a Facility already carries a reservation of Pending or Approved status, no Class Schedule entry conflicting with that reservation shall be admitted into the system until the conflicting reservation has first been resolved — whether by rejection, cancellation, or the passage of its own terms. A Class Schedule shall not operate to displace a reservation already made.

---

## ARTICLE VII — DISPOSITION OF CONFLICTING REQUESTS

**Section 1.** A request that conflicts, at the time of its submission, with an existing Pending or Approved reservation, a Class Schedule, the hours prescribed in Article V, or a date entered in the Holidays table, shall be refused at the point of submission and shall not be entered into the system as a record. Such a refusal does not constitute a "Rejection" within the meaning of this Order.

**Section 2.** "Rejection" refers exclusively to a determination made by an Approving Authority upon a request that was successfully submitted and remains in Pending status.

**Section 3.** As between two requests for the same Facility and time that do not conflict with one another at submission, priority shall belong to whichever request is first successfully recorded by the system.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — APPROVAL PROCESS

**Section 1.** Every reservation request shall be entered in Pending status and shall require the action of an Approving Authority before it may become Approved.

**Section 2.** An Approving Authority's determination shall be final and entire; no partial approval — as by the shortening of a requested time window — shall be made. A request shall be either Approved as submitted, or Rejected.

**Section 3.** A request shall be re-examined against the whole of Articles V through VII at the moment of its approval, and not merely at the moment of its submission. Should circumstances have changed in the interim — as by the addition of a Holiday or a Class Schedule — approval shall be refused notwithstanding the request's earlier eligibility.

---

## ARTICLE IX — RESCHEDULING OF APPROVED RESERVATIONS

**Section 1.** A reservation once Approved may not have its date or hour altered directly by its Requestor. Any such alteration shall proceed only by way of a Move Request, as follows:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) the Requestor shall submit a proposed new start and end time;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) the Approving Authority shall examine the proposal against Articles V through VII;
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) the Approving Authority shall either approve the proposal, whereupon the original reservation's time shall be so amended, or reject it, whereupon the original reservation shall remain unaltered.

**Section 2.** A rejection under Section 1(c) shall state its reason. No Move Request shall be rejected without comment, the original reservation being left standing thereby and the Requestor being entitled to know why.

**Section 3.** No reservation shall carry more than one Move Request in Pending status at a given time. A further Move Request shall not be admitted while an earlier one remains undetermined.

**Section 4.** A Move Request may be submitted upon a reservation in either Pending or Approved status.

---

## ARTICLE X — CANCELLATION

**Section 1.** A reservation in Pending status may be cancelled by its Requestor directly and without delay, at any time, without need of approval by any Approving Authority.

**Section 2.** A reservation in Approved status may not be cancelled directly by its Requestor. Cancellation of such a reservation shall proceed only by way of a Cancellation Request, stating the reason therefor, which shall be examined and either approved or rejected by an Approving Authority in the same manner as a Move Request. A rejection shall state its reason, the reservation being left standing thereby.

**Section 2-A.** Where a Cancellation Request is approved, the reservation shall thereupon pass to Cancelled status and the Facility shall be released. The reason recorded against the reservation shall be that stated by the Requestor.

**Section 2-B.** No reservation shall carry more than one Cancellation Request in Pending status at a given time.

**Section 3.** No Cancellation Request upon an Approved reservation shall be admitted for submission on or after the calendar date of that reservation's scheduled commencement. Where the day of the reservation has arrived, the reservation shall run its course; any failure of the Requestor to appear shall be recorded thereafter by an Approving Authority as a separate matter, and shall not be treated as a cancellation.

**Section 4.** Nothing in this Article shall be read to prevent an Approving Authority from cancelling an Approved reservation directly, upon its own discretion, without recourse to the Cancellation Request procedure.

---

## ARTICLE XI — ADDITIONAL CONDITIONS ON ALL REQUESTS

**Section 1.** No reservation request shall be admitted whose proposed start time has already passed.

**Section 2.** No reservation request shall be admitted whose proposed start and end fall upon different calendar dates.

---

## ARTICLE XII — RETENTION AND VISIBILITY OF RECORDS

**Section 1.** A Requestor may remove a reservation of Pending, Rejected, or Cancelled status from their own view of their reservation history. Such removal shall affect visibility alone; the underlying record shall not be deleted, and shall remain preserved in full for the purposes of audit.

---

## ARTICLE XIII — COMPLETION AND RE-FILING OF RESERVATIONS

**Section 1.** A reservation whose scheduled period has fully elapsed shall be marked Completed by an Approving Authority. The system performs no such marking of its own accord; until the act is taken, an elapsed reservation remains in the status it last held.

**Section 2.** A reservation of Completed, Rejected, or Cancelled status, and no other, may be used as the basis of a new request, carrying forward its Facility, classification, and description, subject in every respect to the whole of this Order as though newly filed. A reservation still Pending or Approved shall not be so used, the Facility being at that time already held.

**Section 3.** The procedure described in Section 2 of this Article is the sole means by which a Staff or Admin account may cause a reservation to be filed, and shall in every instance be recorded under the original Requestor pursuant to Article II, Section 2.

---

## ARTICLE XIV — ACCOUNTABILITY

**Section 1.** Every reservation shall belong to exactly one Requestor, who shall be the sole party accountable for the condition of the reserved Facility and for the conduct of all persons, including guests, present therein for the duration of the reservation, without regard to its classification under Article III.

---

## ARTICLE XV — LIMITATIONS OF SYSTEM ENFORCEMENT

**Section 1.** The disclosures required under Article III, Section 2 and Article IV, Section 2 are not verified by the system. Their omission does not prevent submission or approval; their accuracy remains a matter for the Approving Authority's own review.

**Section 2.** Registration of new accounts is restricted to University email addresses. This restriction governs the creation of new accounts only; it does not and shall not be construed to affect the standing of any account created prior to the restriction's effectivity, nor does it restrict the authentication of any existing account.

---

## ARTICLE XVI — REPEALING CLAUSE AND EFFECTIVITY

**Section 1.** All prior practices, memoranda, or understandings respecting the reservation of shared campus facilities that are inconsistent with this Order are hereby deemed superseded.

**Section 2.** This Order shall take effect immediately upon its posting within the CampusRoom system.

---

**APPROVED**, this 20th day of September, 2026, at Buenavista, Bohol.
