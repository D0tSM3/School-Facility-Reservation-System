Student & Faculty Guidelines

Reservation Rules & Procedures

Key guidelines on how students and faculty request spaces, select event categories, manage bookings, and access facilities.

1

Browse & Check Live Availability

Students and faculty can query real-time room schedules, seat capacities, and facility features across Building #1545 before filing a request.

2

Select an Event Classification

Every booking must fall under an approved academic category: Academic Lecture, Faculty Defense, Student Org Meeting, Dept Workshop, or Exam/Quiz.

3

Reserve in 30-Minute Blocks

Choose granular 30-minute time intervals within operating hours (Monday–Saturday, 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM). Single-day and multi-day reservations are supported.

4

Specify Equipment & Setup Notes

Request needed equipment (projectors, sound systems, whiteboards, or microphone units) directly in your reservation submission notes.

5

Track Status & Review Remarks

Monitor the live progress of submitted requests from your personal dashboard, including review remarks and official approval notices.

6

Request to Reschedule (Move Requests)

If your schedule or room needs change, submit a Move Request to transfer an approved booking to another available room or date.

7

Cancel or Release Bookings

Easily cancel pending submissions, or request cancellation for approved reservations if an event is called off so the space becomes free for others.

8

Download Official Confirmation Slips

Access verified, printable confirmation slips for all approved reservations to present to building custodians and campus security upon arrival.
