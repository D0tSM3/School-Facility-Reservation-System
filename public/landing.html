Reservation Rules & Procedures

The following rules govern all use of the CampusRoom system, as prescribed by the Office of the University President.

1

Eligibility

Only authenticated Customer accounts (students/faculty) may submit reservations. Staff and Admin accounts cannot reserve in their own name. No public access is permitted.

2

Classifications

Every request must be filed under one classification: Academic Lecture, Faculty Defense, Student Org Meeting, Dept Workshop, or Exam/Quiz. No other classifications are accepted.

3

Operating Hours & Availability

Facilities may only be reserved within configured system hours, on open days (Mon–Sat), excluding holidays. No extensions are permitted. Rooms under maintenance are completely unavailable.

4

Class Schedule Precedence

Academic class schedules take absolute priority. A facility committed to a class schedule cannot be reserved during that time by any requestor, without exception.

5

Conflict Prevention

Requests conflicting with existing Pending/Approved reservations, class schedules, holidays, or closed hours are refused at submission. Priority belongs to whichever request was recorded first.

6

Approval Process

All requests enter Pending status and require Approving Authority action. Approval is all-or-nothing — no partial approvals (e.g., shortening a time window). Re-validation occurs at the moment of approval.

7

Rescheduling & Cancellation

Approved reservations cannot be directly altered. Changes require a Move Request reviewed by the Approving Authority. Cancellation of approved reservations requires a Cancellation Request — except on the day of the event.

8

Accountability & Records

Every reservation belongs to exactly one Requestor, who is accountable for the facility and all attendees including guests. All records are preserved for audit even when hidden from the requestor's view.
